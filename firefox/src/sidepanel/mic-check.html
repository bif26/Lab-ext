🎙 LanguageShadow – Microphone

Recording your voice needs a one-time microphone permission from the browser. Audio stays on your device: it is only sent to your local scoring server (127.0.0.1:8000) when you press Score on a take.

If the permission stays blocked:
Chrome: open chrome://settings/content/microphone → make sure this extension is not in the “Not allowed” list, then reload the side panel.
Firefox: open about:preferences#privacy → Permissions → Microphone → Settings… → remove any blocked entry, or allow the prompt when it appears at the top-left of the window.
